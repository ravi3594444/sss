Welcome to Karnataka Cafe

Dine Amidst Nature's Tranquil Beauty & Warm Serenity

Born from over 15 years of passion for authentic South Indian food, Karnataka Cafe presents a refined interpretation of South Indian cuisine, marrying traditional flavors with modern culinary elegance.

Step into our Patna outlet, where warm ambient hospitality envelops you. Every morning, our chefs prepare batter fresh, roast dosas to a golden crisp and brew filter coffee the traditional way.

  • 100% fresh batter & brass-style authentic preparations
  • Authentic regional recipes from Karnataka, Andhra, Malabar & Tamil Nadu
  • Highest food safety & hygiene standards
  • Warm family-friendly fine dining experience
