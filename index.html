Welcome to Karnataka Cafe

Bringing the True Taste of Karnataka to Patna

Born from over 15 years of passion for authentic South Indian food, Karnataka Cafe has grown from a single outlet into a beloved food revolution. Our Patna franchise brings the same legacy of flavour, freshness and tradition right to your neighbourhood.

Every morning, our chefs prepare batter fresh, roast dosas to a perfect golden crisp and brew filter coffee the traditional way — so that every visit tastes like home.

  • 100% fresh batter & ingredients prepared daily
  • Authentic recipes passed down through generations
  • Hygienic kitchen with the highest food-safety standards
  • A warm, family-friendly dining experience
